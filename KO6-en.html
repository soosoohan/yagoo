Hangul Baseball (easy)

Hangul Baseball is a logic word puzzle inspired by the classic Bulls and Cows game, adapted for learning Korean vocabulary.

How to Play & Rules

In this easy version, every secret word is made of simple syllables without final consonants (batchim), so it suits beginners and language learners.

How to Play Soosoo Hangul Baseball

How to Play Hangul Baseball (Easy)

To keep the keyboard simple, you only enter consonants. The vowels of the secret word are provided as hints, so you can focus on finding the right consonants.

Game Rules & Hints

  • Strike (S): a consonant is correct and in the exact position.
  • Ball (B): a consonant is in the secret word, but in the wrong position.
  • Out (O): a consonant does not appear in the secret word at all.

The game ends when you guess the word or use up all attempts. All answers are nouns with 2 to 4 syllables, and only words in the word pool can be used.
